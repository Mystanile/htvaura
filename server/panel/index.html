Scenario guide

"User" is the phone that started the night. "Friend" is a phone in tier 1. Each step names a phone action or a panel button.

1. Safe
  1. Start a night on the user's phone. Put a friend in tier 1.
  2. On the user's card, press Trigger "manual".
  3. On the user's phone, tap three times quickly to say "I'm okay".
  4. The card goes back to armed. The privacy view shows that nobody sees an alert.
2. No response
  1. On the user's card, press Trigger "manual".
  2. Do not touch the user's phone. Wait 45 seconds, or press +60 s on the top bar once.
  3. The card shows escalating. The alert lists the tier 1 friend as alerted.
  4. The friend's phone shows the alert and its response card.
3. Duress
  1. On the user's card, press Trigger "manual".
  2. On the user's phone, hold for 1 second, then tap twice quickly. The phone shows the same "Thanks" screen as a normal "I'm okay".
  3. On the panel, the alert is marked "Hidden: the user's phone shows nothing". Only the duress contacts are alerted.
  4. In the privacy view, group members and tier contacts show "no". Duress contacts show "yes (duress)".
4. Connectivity failure
  1. On the user's card, press Trigger "manual".
  2. Press "Disconnect phone". The badge turns to "offline".
  3. Do not answer. Press +60 s on the top bar once.
  4. The server still escalates. The friend's phone gets the alert while the user's phone is offline.
5. Unacknowledged alert
  1. Start an alert as in scenario 2. The tier 1 friend gets it.
  2. The tier 1 friend does not tap "I'm on it". Press +30 s on the top bar.
  3. The alert moves to tier 2. The list shows both tiers as alerted.
  4. On a tier 2 phone, tap "I'm on it". The alert shows who acknowledged.
6. False alarm
  1. Start an alert as in scenario 2.
  2. Option A: on the user's phone, enter the right PIN to end the alert. The alert shows resolved.
  3. Option B: on the phone of the friend who tapped "I'm on it", tap resolve. The alert shows resolved.
  4. Wrong PIN: the first wrong PIN shows "try again". The second one shows "Alert ended" on the user's phone, but the panel still shows the alert running, marked hidden.
7. Privacy
  1. With the night armed, press "Set sample location" on the user's card. The privacy view shows "no" in the location column for everyone.
  2. Press Trigger "manual" and skip 60 s once. Tier contacts now show location "yes".
  3. If the night was started with share_location_with set to "acknowledger", tap "I'm on it" on one phone. Only that person shows "yes".
  4. Resolve the alert. Everyone goes back to "no". Group members never saw the duress alert in scenario 3.
